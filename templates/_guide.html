Length
8, 16 or 32 bars. Only 32 bars gets a break, a peak and an outro.
Entanglement
How much the qubits depend on each other. Low: every hit is on its own, melodies wander, and the chord walk marches straight round the ring. High: the bass follows the kick, melodies keep moving the same way, and the chord walk doubles back on itself.
Interference
Lets amplitudes add or cancel, which is the part ordinary randomness can't do. It moves ghost notes around the bar and shapes melodies into rising and falling arcs.
Decoherence
Adds noise to the gates, like a real noisy quantum computer. Turn it up and the interference washes out, the groove loosens, and the piece falls apart.
Seed
Names one run. The same settings and seeds always make the same piece. The shuffle button picks a new seed.

Tracing a note

Click a note and the bar under the arrangement tells you which qubit decided it, in which shot, and how often that qubit measured 1. Press Show in circuit to jump to that qubit's gates.

Reading the circuit

A circuit is read left to right, and each horizontal line is one qubit. The band across the top names the stages: Prepare, Entangle, Interfere and Measure. The groove starts with a long column of Ry gates because every qubit gets prepared first, so scroll right or press See it all. Hover over any gate to see what it does there.

GateWhat it doesIn the music
RyRotates a qubit, which sets its chance of measuring 1.How likely a kick, hat or note is on this step.
Dot and RyA controlled rotation: only rotates if the qubit with the dot is 1. Blue raises the chance; red lowers it. This is what entangles qubits.A kick makes the bass more likely. A note makes the next note less likely.
RzAdds a phase. That changes nothing you could measure on its own, until the next rotation turns it into interference.Moves ghost notes around, and shapes the melody's arcs.
MMeasures: the qubit collapses to 0 or 1.Each set of bits becomes a bar or a motif.
+1 and −1Moves the walker around the ring, depending on the coin qubit.Which chord comes next.
Z, S, X, PSmaller steps in the chord walk: the coin flip, its balanced start, the starting chord, and a phase around the ring.They shape which chords are likely.

How qubits make music

Groove: 80 qubits, one for each kick, snare, hat, bass and chord stab position in a bar. Each measurement shot gives one bar, so the bars vary but keep the same feel.

Melody: 96 qubits. For each 16th note, three qubits decide whether a note starts, which way it moves, and whether it leaps. Each shot is a two-bar motif, arranged so that ideas come back.

Chords: a quantum walk around the circle of fifths. Each walk starts on the previous chord and lands on the next one.

Music theory handles the rest: staying in key, landing strong beats on chord tones, and keeping chords smooth. Nothing else rolls dice. These qubits are simulated on a normal computer with Qiskit Aer, not run on quantum hardware.

If something's wrong

Compose takes a long time the first time
The free server goes to sleep when nobody's using it, and can take a minute to wake. After that it's quick. The loaded piece plays in the meantime.
No sound
Press Play first, because browsers won't start audio on their own. Then check the Volume slider, your device's volume, and whether a part is muted or another is soloed.
The sound crackles
Your computer may be busy. Close other tabs and press Play again.
